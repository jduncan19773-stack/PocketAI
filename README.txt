============================================================
  PocketAI — Your Private AI Assistant
============================================================

FIRST TIME? Run this once:
  -> Double-click SETUP.bat
     (downloads the AI software, takes 10-20 minutes)

EVERY OTHER TIME:
  -> Double-click START_POCKETAI.bat
     (your browser opens automatically in a few seconds)

THAT'S IT. No typing, no installation, no internet needed.

------------------------------------------------------------
TIPS:
  * Type your question and press Enter (or click Send)
  * Click the microphone button (top right of text box) to speak
  * Click the speaker button to hear responses read aloud
  * Use "New Chat" to start a fresh conversation
  * Use "+ " in "Things AI Remembers" to save personal facts
    (the AI will remember them in every future conversation)

------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS POCKETAI?
  PocketAI is a private AI assistant that runs entirely on
  this flash drive. It never sends your data to the internet.
  Everything stays on this drive. Unplug it and the computer
  is left completely clean — nothing installed, nothing stored.
